# Chotu voice commands

Open Chotu from POS. Its microphone starts after sign-in and business access
are verified. Select Hindi, Hinglish or English if recognition needs a different
language. Type the same commands if the microphone is unavailable.

- “Table five pe do butter naan laga do.”
- “Butter naan ko four kar do.”
- “K O T print karo.” / “के ओ टी प्रिंट करो।”
- “Bill bana do.” (unpaid invoice)
- “Settle bill UPI.” (asks whether the full bill total was received)
- “Haan confirm.” (records the selected payment against the unchanged bill)
- “Card payment received, settle bill.” / “कैश पेमेंट मिल गया सेटल करो।”
- “Cancel.” (cancels the pending confirmation)

Use an enabled Cash, UPI or Card method. Confirm UPI/Card only after checking
actual receipt; Chotu records the payment and does not collect or bank-verify it.
Split/partial payments, new product variants and special instructions use the
existing POS. An already-issued manual bill is settled at its stored prices and
totals. A changed or ambiguous draft is rejected for reconciliation.

KOT/bills use the saved printer. An uncertain printer result leaves the order
saved; check the device and reprint through Orders rather than repeating payment.

In Chotu's tune menu choose “Hello Chotu · 2 min in app” to arm temporary standby.
Say only “Hello Chotu”, then give the order after the overlay opens. A visible
Stop chip ends standby. Backgrounding or locking stops listening.

For supported Android shortcuts, choose “Android assistant settings” and select
Apna POS as the digital assistant. The phone's configured assistant gesture
(power hold or home gesture, depending on OEM) then opens Chotu after unlock.
Always-on background/screen-off Hello Chotu is not included in this release.
